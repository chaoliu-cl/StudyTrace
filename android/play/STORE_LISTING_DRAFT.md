# Google Play listing draft

## App name

StudyTrace

## Short description

Join approved research studies and securely contribute phone-use and survey data.

## Full description

StudyTrace is a research participation app used by universities, laboratories,
and other approved research teams. Participants join a specific study using the
secure URL supplied by their research team and review that study's consent terms
before collection begins.

Depending on the study and the permissions you choose to grant, StudyTrace can:

- deliver scheduled research surveys;
- measure app foreground-use duration and screen lock or unlock events;
- collect location for mobility and context research, including in the background;
- record device and synchronization status needed to operate the study; and
- securely upload study data to the research server configured by your study.

StudyTrace collects location data to enable research mobility and context sensing
even when the app is closed or not in use. Background location is collected only
after you join a study, view the in-app disclosure, and grant Android permission.
A persistent notification shows when location collection is active. You can stop
location collection, revoke permissions, or leave the study at any time.

App-usage collection records package names, app labels, foreground-use duration,
launch counts, and screen-state transitions. It does not collect the content you
view or type inside other apps.

StudyTrace contains no advertising SDK. Study data is used for the purpose
described by the research team and its study consent. Server ownership, retention,
and research contacts vary by study; contact the coordinator who invited you for
study-specific questions.
