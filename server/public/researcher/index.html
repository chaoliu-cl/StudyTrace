ESM survey notifications

Schedule ordinary ESM survey prompts. Fixed mode sends at exact times; random mode jitters around those times by the selected window.

Updated apps start the response window at notification delivery. These hours limit new prompts; a delivered survey can stay open afterward. Leave both hours blank for unrestricted delivery.

Templates are appended to the questions below; nothing changes for participants until you save. Confirm wording with your IRB and cite the listed source.

Photo questions use esm_type: 14; participants can choose multiple existing photos or take pictures. Up to 10 images per question (12 MiB combined). Optional quality fields such as studytrace_required, studytrace_branching, and studytrace_randomize_options are preserved in the schedule JSON for analysis and future client support.

Environment collection

        
GPS-linked outdoor weather and daylight timing, with optional air pollution, map context, and brief survey context questions. Personal light and indoor exposure sensing are off. The master control must be On for the selected options.

        

          
          
          
          
          
Context adds no notifications. Three questionnaires per day add 15 study-specific items; original instrument wording and choices stay unchanged. Map context is historical land cover, not proof of a current park visit.

          
        

        

        

          Measures, accuracy, and participant information
          
Weather includes temperature (°C), humidity (%), apparent temperature (°C), wind (m/s), precipitation (mm), cloud cover (%), weather code, and solar radiation (W/m²), hourly after location uploads. Sunrise, sunset, and day length use the phone's local date, or study time zone if unavailable. Open-Meteo receives coordinates rounded to 0.01°.

          
Air pollution adds PM2.5, PM10, ozone, nitrogen dioxide, sulfur dioxide, carbon monoxide (µg/m³), and US/EU AQI. These are outdoor model estimates; global model resolution is approximately 45 km and hourly values can interpolate coarser model times.

          
Context questions ask about current setting, perceived noise, temperature sensation, recent outdoor time, and green-space contact. They are optional participant reports, not validated scale scores. No microphone audio or decibels are collected.

          
Maps provide land-cover percentages within 300 m, at most once per 15-minute period after uploads. The map's reference year is 2021 and nominal resolution is 10 m. GPS accuracy must be 100 m or better; poor/missing accuracy and map gaps are flagged instead of zero greenery. The server reads map tiles and calculates the buffer. Include provider processing and new items in participant information/consent.

        

        
Weather, daylight, air quality, and maps appear in environment_measures; context answers appear in survey_responses. Missing data stay missing. Weather: Open-Meteo.com, CC BY 4.0. Air quality: CAMS ENSEMBLE providers via Open-Meteo. Maps: © ESA WorldCover project 2021 / Contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data (2021), CC BY 4.0. Include this collection in study consent.

Daily phone use

            
phone_use_daily, derived from lock/unlock events (iOS plugin_device_usage, Android android_screen_events) and split at the participant's local midnight. Counts observed unlock sessions and session lengths; it does not identify apps or notifications. Night use is 00:00–05:00 local. On iOS these events are only recorded while StudyTrace is running, so check participant health for gaps.

Battery screenshot app usage

            
Participants upload screenshots from Settings → Battery → View All Battery Usage. Extracts per-app on-screen time and battery percentage over the selected Battery window (often a rolling 24 hours), which differs from calendar-day Screen Time. Each screenshot is kept separately; overlapping captures must not be summed.

          

        

        

          

        

        

          

        

      


      

        

          

            
Screen Time activity

            
screen_time_activity: one daily-total row (screen time, pickups, notifications) plus one row per most-used app for each screenshot. These calendar-day or today-so-far snapshots differ from observed unlock sessions and rolling Battery windows. Multiple screenshots can cover different parts of one screen; do not sum repeated totals.
