Participant health

Operational data-quality checks from client events, device state, uploads, permissions, and recent study rows. Compliance is answered survey sessions divided by survey prompts the phone reported as delivered over the last 7 days (prompts swiped away before the app next opened may be missed).

ESM survey notifications

Schedule ordinary ESM survey prompts. Fixed mode sends at exact times; random mode jitters around those times by the selected window.

Optional quality fields such as studytrace_required, studytrace_branching, and studytrace_randomize_options are preserved in the schedule JSON for analysis and future client support.

Daily phone use

            
phone_use_daily, derived from lock/unlock events (iOS plugin_device_usage, Android android_screen_events) and split at the participant's local midnight. Night use is 00:00–05:00 local. On iOS these events are only recorded while StudyTrace is running, so check participant health for gaps.
