iPhone enrollment workflow

StudyTrace on iPhone collects only the streams enabled by your research study after consent. Sign in once with a personal invitation from your research team.

1. Install

Open the TestFlight invite from your research team and install the StudyTrace iPhone app.

2. Consent

Complete the study-specific consent with the research team. After verifying it, the team gives you a personal invitation.

3. Sign in once

Scan your personal invitation in StudyTrace. An internet connection verifies your consent access and binds it to this device. No password creation or daily sign-in is required.

What participants should expect

  • Location collection only if your study enables it and you grant permission.
  • Survey prompts and optional photo attachments when your protocol uses them.
  • Optional Battery usage screenshot uploads if your study asks you to share iOS app-usage context.
  • Settings → Quit Study stops collection and notifies the study server when connected.

App-usage screenshot collection is optional and participant-driven. If your study requests it, the app asks you to open Settings → Battery → View All Battery Usage, take a screenshot, and upload that screenshot as a survey photo response.

Study URL format

Researchers distribute URLs like the example below. The app should receive this inside StudyTrace, not in Safari.

https://your-study.up.railway.app/index.php/webservice/index/pilot1/pi_PERSONAL_INVITATION_TOKEN?participant=P001
