Privacy for StudyTrace

Effective date: October 1, 2026. This policy explains what the StudyTrace iPhone app collects, where it goes, and the choices you have. Each study you join also shows you its own consent form, written by the research team, before anything is collected.

1. Overview

StudyTrace is an app for taking part in research studies run by universities and research teams. You join a study with a link or QR code from the research team. Before you join, StudyTrace shows that study's consent form: its purpose, how long it lasts, what you will do, its risks and benefits, how your data is protected, who to contact, how to withdraw, and the ethics board that approved it. Nothing is collected unless you agree.

The research team that runs your study decides what the study collects and is responsible for the study data on its server. Questions about a specific study should go to the contact listed in that study's consent form.

2. Data StudyTrace Collects

After you agree to a study's consent, and depending on what that study uses and the permissions you allow:

  • Study enrollment: the study you joined, a participant code if the research team put one in your link, and a record of which consent version you agreed to and when.
  • A device ID: a random identifier created by the app, so the research team can tell participants' data apart. It is not your name, phone number, or Apple ID.
  • Location: GPS location, including in the background, if the study collects location and you allow it.
  • Phone use timing: when your screen is locked and unlocked, used to measure daily phone use.
  • Survey answers: your answers to the study's surveys, including any photo you choose to attach.
  • Screenshots you upload: if the study asks, screenshots of iPhone Settings → Battery or Settings → Screen Time that you choose to upload. The phone reads the text in the screenshot and uploads that text with the image; the study server extracts app names, usage times, pickups, and notification counts from it. The image is re-saved without the original file's location or device details; only the time it was taken is kept.
  • App and device diagnostics: app version, iOS version, iPhone model, battery level and charging state, Low Power Mode, time zone, which permissions are allowed, and app events such as when surveys were delivered or opened and whether uploads succeeded. The research team uses these to check that the study is running properly.

3. Data StudyTrace Does Not Collect

  • Your name, email address, or phone number (unless you type them into a survey answer).
  • Contacts, calendars, health or fitness data, motion and activity data, microphone audio, or Bluetooth data.
  • Apple's Screen Time database. App-usage information comes only from screenshots you choose to upload.
  • The advertising identifier. StudyTrace contains no advertising or third-party analytics code and does not track you across apps or websites.

4. Permissions

StudyTrace asks for permissions only after you agree to a study's consent:

  • Location, to record your location for studies that collect it. You can choose "While Using the App", "Always", or "Don't Allow"; the rest of the study still works if you decline.
  • Notifications, to remind you when a survey is available. Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone.
  • Camera, only when you scan a study QR code or take a photo for a survey answer.
  • Photos, to let you pick a picture or screenshot to attach. You choose each image; StudyTrace does not read the rest of your library.

You can change these at any time in iPhone Settings → StudyTrace.

5. How Data Is Used

  • To run the study you joined: deliver surveys and reminders and upload your study data.
  • For the research described in that study's consent form.
  • To help the research team check that data is being collected and uploaded correctly.

Study data is not sold, used for advertising, or used to build profiles for anyone outside the research described in your consent.

6. Where Data Goes

Data is kept on your iPhone until it is uploaded, then sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to the server of the study you joined. The consent screen shows that server's address. The research team accesses the data through a password-protected dashboard. The StudyTrace app developer does not receive your study data unless they also run that study's server.

The research team may use service providers (for example, cloud hosting) to run their server. How long data is kept, and anyone else it is shared with, is described in your study's consent form.

7. Leaving a Study and Deleting Data

You can leave a study at any time: open StudyTrace, go to Settings, and tap Leave Study. Leaving stops all collection, cancels survey reminders, and deletes the study data stored on your iPhone, including data not yet uploaded. You then choose whether the study server should also delete the data your iPhone already uploaded, or keep it for the study as described in the consent form. If the server cannot be reached, StudyTrace retries automatically.

You can also contact the research team listed in your consent form about your data. Deleting the app stops collection but does not by itself delete data already uploaded, so use Leave Study first if you want it deleted.

8. Children

StudyTrace is not directed to children. A study that includes minors must obtain consent from a parent or guardian as required by its ethics board.

9. Changes to This Policy

We will update the effective date above when this policy changes. If a research team changes its study's consent, StudyTrace asks you to read and agree to the new version before continuing.

10. Contact

Questions about the StudyTrace app or this policy: chaoliu@cedarville.edu.

Questions about a specific study, your study data, or withdrawing: contact the research team listed in that study's consent form.
