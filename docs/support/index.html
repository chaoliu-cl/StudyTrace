App support

Trouble joining, uploading, or receiving reminders? Email chaoliu@cedarville.edu with your iPhone model, iOS version, and roughly when the problem happened. Your Device ID (StudyTrace → Settings) helps too.

Questions about your study

Surveys, payments, what a study collects, or how your data is used: contact the research team listed under "Questions about the study" in the consent form you agreed to.

Leaving and deleting data

Open StudyTrace → Settings → Leave Study. You can choose to have the data your iPhone uploaded deleted from the study server.

FAQ

Common participant questions

How do I join a study?

Open the study link from your research team on your iPhone, or open StudyTrace and tap Join a Study to scan its QR code or paste the link. StudyTrace shows the study's consent form; the study starts only if you agree.

Why does the app ask for location, notifications, camera, or photos?

Only for what your study uses: location for studies that measure mobility, notifications for survey reminders, the camera to scan a study QR code or take a survey photo, and photos so you can pick a screenshot or picture to attach. You can decline any of them.

Does StudyTrace read my Screen Time automatically?

No. If your study asks for app-usage information, StudyTrace shows which iPhone Settings screen to capture (Battery or Screen Time), and you choose a screenshot to upload.

How do I upload a Battery or Screen Time screenshot?

When your study asks, an upload button appears on the Surveys tab. Tap it, follow the steps to take the screenshot in iPhone Settings, then come back and choose it. If the screenshot is hard to read, StudyTrace asks you to retake it.

Can I stop participating?

Yes, at any time. Open Settings in StudyTrace and tap Leave Study. Collection stops, the study data on your iPhone is deleted, and you choose whether the data already uploaded is deleted too.

Why am I not receiving survey reminders?

Check that notifications are allowed for StudyTrace in iPhone Settings, and ask your research team whether your study currently has surveys scheduled.

What if the app does not upload or seems inactive?

Open the app with an internet connection and check that Settings shows your study. Uploads that failed are retried automatically. If the problem continues, email support with the approximate time it happened.
