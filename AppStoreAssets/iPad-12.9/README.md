# StudyTrace App Store assets — iPad 12.9-inch

Generated from the iPad Pro (12.9-inch, 6th generation) simulator on October 1, 2026. All study and participant details are fictional.

## Upload-ready assets

- `screenshots/`: 10 opaque RGB PNG files, each 2048 × 2732 pixels.
- `previews/`: 3 portrait H.264 MP4 files, each 1200 × 1600 pixels, 30 fps, 18.47 seconds, progressive High Profile Level 4.0, and approximately 9.9 Mbps. The previews have no audio track and are designed for muted autoplay.

The numeric filename prefixes are the intended App Store display order.

## Preview themes

1. `01-onboarding-consent.mp4` — research purpose, data scope, researcher setup, and informed consent.
2. `02-study-dashboard.mp4` — scheduled prompts, participant dashboard, study status, and privacy controls.
3. `03-survey-uploads.mp4` — participant-controlled Battery and Screen Time screenshot workflows.

## Capture support

The app recognizes `-AppStoreDemo`, `-AppStoreDemoOnboarding`, `-AppStorePage`, `-AppStoreScene`, and `-AppStorePreview` only when explicitly passed as process launch arguments. Normal launches never enable the fictional capture data.

`raw-video/` contains source simulator recordings and is not intended for App Store upload.

Specifications were checked against Apple’s [screenshot specifications](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications/) and [app preview specifications](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-preview-specifications).
