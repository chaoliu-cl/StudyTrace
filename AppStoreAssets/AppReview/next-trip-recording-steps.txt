StudyTrace build 11 — recording procedure

Purpose: demonstrate real mobility-history collection while StudyTrace stays
in the background. The October 20 trip recording has now been retrieved and
validated; these steps are retained for future recordings. The validated
deliverable is background-location-iPhone-build11.mp4.

Before leaving

1. Temporarily set Settings > Display & Brightness > Auto-Lock to Never so
   the screen recording will not stop when the phone locks.
2. Start the iPhone's built-in Screen Recording from Control Center.
3. Show Settings > Apps > StudyTrace > Location with Always selected for
   about five seconds.
4. Open StudyTrace > Dashboard, tap the upper-right refresh icon, and leave
   the Location map visible for about five seconds.
5. Swipe Home. Keep StudyTrace running in the background without force-quitting
   it. You can now disconnect the USB cable.

During the trip

Keep the recording running continuously and the screen awake. Stay outside
StudyTrace for 6–10 minutes while travelling, reaching roughly 500 meters away
from the starting point. Avoid a small loop around the starting point. The app
uses a 300-meter movement filter and a three-minute sampling rate limit, so
both movement and elapsed time matter. Do not reopen StudyTrace mid-trip.

At the end

1. Reopen StudyTrace > Dashboard and tap refresh. Leave the Location map
   visible for about 10 seconds.
2. Stop Screen Recording and keep the original video in Photos.
3. Restore your preferred Auto-Lock setting.
4. Reconnect and unlock the iPhone, then reply "trip complete".

Validation afterward

The recording alone will not establish when a point was saved. We will match
the on-device location rows to receipt events showing background application
state, fresh fix times, and delivery clearly before the app was reopened. Only
after that check will a video be prepared as App Review evidence. The existing
stationary/walking diagnostic videos are not suitable submission evidence.
